## Overload protection

| Check | Setting | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Hard limit | `MAX_SESSIONS=9`, 12 candidates | 9 ran (all answers replied to, p95 5.58 s over 3 answers each), 3 refused as full |
| Slow replies | threshold lowered to 3 s on purpose, 3 candidates, then one more | `/health` showed `accepting: false, busy_reason: slow`, the new candidate was refused, and tests were accepted again when checked 75 s later |
| Slow replies at the shipped threshold (12 s) | the 32 run | **Nobody was refused**: the server's own delay measure reached p95 6.48 s while candidates waited 13.3 s |
| Low GPU memory (under 1500 MB free) | not reached | not verified on a GPU |
| Restart when memory is not given back | not triggered | not verified on a GPU (see below) |
| Run after the overload | 6 candidates right after the 32 run | PASS, p95 5.97 s, 21.2 GB |

## Part 2 fix and single save, checked on this GPU

- A candidate talked for 38 s with no pause longer than 0.24 s: the examiner said nothing during the talk (147 words transcribed). The next
  examiner line came 5.7 s after the candidate stopped.
- After the first ramp: 65 saved records for 65 different tests (one for the Part 2 check, 64 from the ramp).

## What we found

1. **No answer went unanswered at any level, including 32.** On 2026-11-24 about half had no reply at 32. The cause of the difference was not
   isolated (another host, and the code changed in between).
2. **12 at once passed the 1-second rule this time; 16 did not.** 12 passed by a small margin and the 9 run was slightly slower than the 12
   run, so differences of a few tenths of a second are noise. **`MAX_SESSIONS=9` is still the recommended setting, 12 the upper end.**
3. **The hard session limit is the protection that works today.** `setup_instance.sh` starts the service with the default of 6; set 9.
4. **The slow-reply threshold is too high to help.** It watches the server's measure, which stayed near half the 12 s limit even at 32.
   Lowering it is not clearly useful either: that measure moved only from 4.45 s (1 candidate) to 6.48 s (32).
5. **GPU memory is still not given back after load** (17.5 GB at the start of the ramp, 19.9 GB after 20; 21.1 GB after the 32 and 6 runs). A
   restart brought it to 17.1 GB. The automatic restart did not trigger: after a restart followed at once by load, its baseline was first
   measured after the load (20.0 GB), so the trigger point was too high.
6. Live captions were skipped at times while the GPU was busy, as designed.
