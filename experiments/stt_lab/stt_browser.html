STT Accuracy Lab — Whisper in the browser

Record your voice or upload audio, type what was actually said as the reference, and the page computes WER/CER and shows exactly which words were missed, changed or added. Audio never leaves your computer (only the model files are downloaded).
This runs OpenAI Whisper weights through ONNX/WebAssembly. The planned server engine is faster-whisper (same Whisper models, different runtime), so accuracy by model size carries over; speed numbers do not.
